# Artifact template · Judgment coaching plan

The tutor fills this in with the learner, for one real person and one real
decision that is live this week. Never invent what the other person said, and
never improve the learner's remembered wording of their own past questions.
Where the learner cannot reconstruct something, write `<unknown>` and carry it
into the Gaps section.

This artifact is used in a conversation, not filed. Question wording must be
verbatim. A plan that says "ask about the evidence base" will not survive the
moment of pressure, which is exactly when a leader reverts to giving the answer.

Delete the guidance in parentheses in the finished artifact.

```markdown
# Judgment coaching plan · <person or role> · <decision>

- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **The decision they hold:** <stated as a choice, with its alternatives>
- **Their current position:** <in their words, not improved>
- **Authority level (LD-02):** <1-5> — <does the call stay with them after this
  conversation? yes / no>
- **Will this decision class recur?** <yes / no — if no, coaching may be the
  wrong use of the time>

## The weak joint

- **Weakest exposure:** <choice | evidence | uncertainty | alternatives | owner
  | revision trigger>
- **Why it is weak, in one sentence:** <specific, not "it is thin">
- **What they hold that I do not:** <the information advantage they have — the
  questions should use it>

## Questions, in order

| # | Verbatim question | Answerable from what they hold? | Which exposure it touches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "<exact words>" | <yes / by when> | <exposure> |
| 2 | "<exact words>" | <yes / by when> | <exposure> |
| 3 | "<exact words>" | <yes / by when> | <exposure> |

## What each answer means

| # | A good answer sounds like | A defensive answer sounds like | My next move in each case |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <…> | <…> | <the follow-up, written out — not "probe further"> |
| 2 | <…> | <…> | <…> |
| 3 | <…> | <…> | <…> |

## Rejected question

<The question you were tempted to ask that is your verdict with a question mark
attached. Write it here and leave it marked rejected. Naming your own
counterfeit is the point.>

- **Tempting question:** "<exact words>" — **rejected because** <…>

## If the reasoning still does not meet the standard

- **Does the call stay with them?** <yes / no>
- **If no, the exact sentence I will say:** "<plain words naming the takeback and
  the reason. If you cannot write it without softening it, you are planning a
  disguised takeback.>"
- **What I will not do:** <the supportive-sounding move that would quietly
  relocate the decision — "let's decide it together", "send it over and I'll
  finish the analysis", "run the final version past me">

## My own exposure move

- **Live decision I will narrate this week:** <a decision of mine, before it is
  resolved>
- **The part I am unsure about:** <stated as I will say it>
- **A past call I got wrong:** <what I knew at the time, not the moral>

## Dependence test

- **Review date:** <YYYY-MM-DD, roughly eight weeks out>
- **Judgment transferred if I see:** <the person arrives with the weakest
  exposure in their own case already named>
- **Judgment did not transfer if I see:** <polished arguments brought to me for a
  verdict; or fewer escalations with no sign the reasoning changed>

## Gaps

<Everything marked `<unknown>` above, listed with who could close it.>
```

## Quality bar

Before saving, check all six exposures are present. Name any that are missing
rather than filling them in yourself:

| Exposure | Present when |
|---|---|
| Choice | The decision the other person holds is stated as a choice with live alternatives |
| Evidence | Each question is answerable from something the person actually holds, by a stated date |
| Uncertainty | The plan says what a defensive answer would look like, and admits which read of the person may be wrong |
| Alternatives | The rejected counterfeit question is written down, and the supportive-sounding takeback moves are named as things not to do |
| Owner | The authority level is stated, and the plan says plainly whether the call stays with them |
| Revision trigger | The dependence test has a date and two observable outcomes |

One further test specific to this artifact: read each question aloud. If any of
them would sound like a verdict to the person receiving it, it is one. Rewrite
it or move it to the rejected line.

Tell the learner they can stress-test this with `review-artifact`.
