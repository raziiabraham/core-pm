# Artifact template · Decision-rights map

The tutor fills this in with the learner, using the learner's judgment about
their own team. Never invent a person, a level that was never agreed, or an
evidence standard the learner did not actually state. Where the learner is
reconstructing after the fact, write `<unknown>` and carry it into the Gaps
section. A rights map that records what you meant to say is worse than one that
records what you did say.

Delete the guidance in parentheses in the finished artifact.

```markdown
# Decision-rights map · <team or decision area>

- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Author:** <name or role>
- **Covers the period:** <start> to <end>
- **Voided when:** <the event that makes this whole map wrong>

## Decisions in scope

<Split bundled questions into separate decisions. "Own the migration" is not a
decision; it is three or four.>

| # | Decision, stated as a choice | Authority level (1-5) | Decider | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <choice> | <1 decide alone / 2 decide and inform / 3 decide after named consultation / 4 recommend, leader decides / 5 leader decides> | <person or role> | <reversible / costly / one-way> |
| 2 | <choice> | <level> | <person> | <…> |
| 3 | <choice> | <level> | <person> | <…> |

<Check: if any row says "shared", "joint", or "collaborative", it is not
assigned. Pick a number.>

## Consequence

| # | Who carries the cost if this is wrong | Same as decider? | Channel to object |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <person or role> | <yes / no> | <documented objection to whom, by when — not a veto unless you mean a veto> |

## Evidence standard

<For the main decision. What must be shown before anyone chooses, stated so that
every option on the table could satisfy it.>

Before deciding #<n>, the decider must have:

1. <required input, with its range or basis>
2. <the consequence of failure stated separately from its probability>
3. <one rejected option and the reason>
4. <the observation that would trigger reversal>

**Bias check:** <Can this standard be met by the option you do not prefer? If
not, rewrite it.>

## Escalation boundaries

| # | Observation that sends this back up | Threshold | Sends it to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <observable, not a feeling> | <number or event> | <person> |

## Progressive autonomy

| Decision class | Current level | Moves up when | Moves down when |
|---|---|---|---|
| <class> | <1-5> | <calibration rule: predictions written in advance, outcomes inside stated confidence, over how many decisions> | <the stated condition — write it now, not after the first failure> |

## Specialist authority not delegated

<Judgment this map does not move, because the person receiving authority is not
qualified to hold it. Name who holds it instead.>

- <area> → <person or role>

## Portability check

<Rewrite the map as it will read to someone who joins in three weeks, has no
history with anyone named above, and cannot reach you for two days. Note what
you had to change.>

- **What broke without me in the room:** <…>
- **What I changed as a result:** <…>

## Gaps

<Everything marked `<unknown>` above, listed as open questions with who could
close them and by when.>
```

## Quality bar

Before saving, check all six exposures are present. Name any that are missing
rather than filling them in yourself:

| Exposure | Present when |
|---|---|
| Choice | Every row states a decision as a choice, with a numbered authority level |
| Evidence | The evidence standard is stated in advance and could be met by any option on the table |
| Uncertainty | Reversibility is recorded per decision, and the map names the event that voids it |
| Alternatives | At least one rejected option is required by the evidence standard, and specialist authority that was not delegated is named |
| Owner | Decider and consequence-carrier are distinguished, and the objection channel is named |
| Revision trigger | Escalation boundaries have an observable and a threshold; autonomy moves on a stated rule in both directions |

One further test specific to this artifact: for each row, ask whether the
decider could write down, unaided, the condition under which you would take the
decision back. If they could not, that row is a loan, not a grant.

Tell the learner they can stress-test this with `review-artifact`.
