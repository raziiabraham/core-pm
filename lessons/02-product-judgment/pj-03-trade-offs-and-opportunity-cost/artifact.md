# Artifact template · Trade-off record

The tutor fills this in with the learner, using the learner's judgment and the
learner's evidence. Never invent a number, a quote, or a source. Where something
is unknown, write `<unknown>` and carry it into the Gaps section. A named gap is
a finding. A fabricated one is a liability.

If the learner has already committed to the choice publicly, say so at the top
of the document and label it a rationale rather than a decision record. Do not
let a defence pass as analysis.

Delete the guidance in parentheses in the finished artifact.

```markdown
# Trade-off record · <chosen option> over <displaced option>

- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Product:** <product>
- **Decided before or after commitment:** <before | after — if after, this is a rationale>
- **Owner of the consequence:** <person or role>
- **Specialist authority:** <who holds expertise this choice depends on>

## The options, as thresholds

| Option | What it buys | Amount | Population | By when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <option A> | <change> | <threshold> | <behaviour-and-context boundary> | <horizon> |
| <option B> | <change> | <threshold> | <behaviour-and-context boundary> | <horizon> |

<If you could not write one of these rows, say which and why. An option you
cannot state as a threshold is one you cannot honestly compare.>

## The scarce resource

- **What is actually constrained:** <the specific people, review bandwidth, design attention, or decision-maker — not "engineering capacity">
- **Substitutable?** <can anyone else do this work, and at what cost in time or quality>
- **What else this resource is the only source of:** <the hidden queue behind it>

## The choice

<One sentence. The option being taken.>

## Displaced option

**Stated by the person who loses it:**

<Write the displaced option's case in the words of its advocate. If they would
not recognise it as their argument, rewrite it.>

- **Who was expecting it:** <name or role>
- **When they will be told:** <date>
- **Delay, if this is a sequence:** <approximately N weeks>
- **Cost of that delay:** <magnitude> — borne by <who>
- **Why that cost is accepted:** <one or two sentences>

## Risk

| If this choice is wrong | What gets worse | Who bears it | How early it becomes visible |
|---|---|---|---|
| <failure mode> | <consequence> | <population> | <date or event> |

## Future flexibility

| Option that closes | When it closes | When it reopens | What reopening costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| <option> | <date or event> | <date or event, or "not on this horizon"> | <cost> |

## Reversal cost

- **Cost to switch back in four weeks:** <what is lost — work, momentum, trust, or contract position>
- **Reversibility:** <reversible | costly to reverse | one-way>

## What would flip this

<One thing you could learn in the next two weeks that would change the choice.
Name the observation, not the topic. If nothing would flip it, this was not a
trade-off — it was a foregone conclusion, and you should say so.>

## Gaps

<Everything marked `<unknown>` above, listed as open questions with who could
close them.>
```

## Quality bar

Before saving, check all six exposures are present. Name any that are missing
rather than filling them in yourself:

| Exposure | Present when |
|---|---|
| Choice | One option is taken, and something specific stops being possible |
| Evidence | Both options are stated as thresholds with populations, or the missing one is named |
| Uncertainty | Risk names a failure mode, a bearer, and when it becomes visible |
| Alternatives | The displaced option is written in its advocate's words, with the delay cost and its payer |
| Owner | Consequence owner and specialist authority are distinguished, and the person told about the displacement is named with a date |
| Revision trigger | One observation that would flip the choice, not a topic to monitor |

Tell the learner they can stress-test this with `review-artifact`.
