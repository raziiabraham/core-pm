# Artifact template · Pre-outcome decision review

The tutor fills this in with the learner, using the learner's judgment and the
learner's evidence. Never invent a number, a quote, or a source. Where something
is unknown, write `<unknown>` and carry it into the Gaps section. A named gap is
a finding. A fabricated one is a liability.

Two rules specific to this artifact. First, if the learner cannot recall the
confidence they held at the time, write `<unknown>` — do not help them
reconstruct it. Second, this document must be timestamped and must not be edited
after the outcome is known. Later thinking goes in a new dated section at the
bottom.

Delete the guidance in parentheses in the finished artifact.

```markdown
# Pre-outcome decision review · <short decision name>

- **Review written:** <YYYY-MM-DD> — before the outcome is known
- **Decision made:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Product:** <product>
- **Decider:** <name or role>
- **Owner of the consequence:** <name or role>
- **Earliest date the outcome is readable:** <date>

## The decision as it was framed

<Restate it as it was actually stated at the time, not as you would state it
now. If it was framed as a sequence or a topic rather than a choice, write that
here — it is the first finding.>

**Alternatives that were live at the time:**

1. <option>
2. <option>

## What was knowable, and at what cost

| Item | Available at the time? | Cost to learn it | Was it collected? | Unknown or uncollected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <item> | <yes / no> | <hours, days, or "not inside the window"> | <yes / no> | <unknown / uncollected> |

<An item is "unknown" only if learning it would have cost more than the
decision's consequence, or could not have been done inside the decision window.
Everything else is "uncollected", and uncollected is a process finding.>

**What was skipped, and why:** <cost, time, or comfort — name which>

## Confidence held at the time

- **Stated confidence:** <low | moderate | high | `<unknown>`>
- **Evidence actually held:** <one line>
- **Did the evidence support that word?** <yes / no> — <why>

## Discriminating findings named in advance

- If we found <A>, we would <action>.
- If we found <B>, we would <different action>.

<If none were named at the time, write "none were named" — that is the finding,
and it is more useful than a set written now.>

## Luck and process, sorted in advance

**Outcomes I would call bad luck:**

1. <specific enough that a reader could sort a real result without asking you>
2. <…>

**Outcomes I would call bad process:**

1. <specific — usually something that was uncollected rather than unknown>
2. <…>

<Write both lists before the result. You will not be allowed to move entries
between them afterwards.>

## Dated checkpoint

- **What will be looked at:** <the observable, with its population and window>
- **When:** <date>
- **Result that would indict the reasoning:** <not just a bad number — a result that shows the mechanism was wrong>
- **Result that would mean the reasoning was sound and the world unlucky:** <…>

## What I would change regardless of outcome

<One thing about how this decision was made that you would do differently even
if the result is good. If you cannot name one, the review is probably too kind.>

## Gaps

<Everything marked `<unknown>` above, listed as open questions with who could
close them.>

---

<Add nothing above this line after the outcome is known. Post-outcome notes go
below, under their own date.>
```

## Quality bar

Before saving, check all six exposures are present. Name any that are missing
rather than filling them in yourself:

| Exposure | Present when |
|---|---|
| Choice | The decision is restated as it was framed at the time, including if it was not a real choice |
| Evidence | Every item is sorted into unknown or uncollected, with the cost of learning it |
| Uncertainty | The confidence held at the time is recorded, and judged against the evidence held |
| Alternatives | The live alternatives and the discriminating findings from that moment are named, or their absence is |
| Owner | Decider and consequence owner are distinguished |
| Revision trigger | A dated checkpoint with a result that would indict the reasoning, not merely a bad number |

Tell the learner they can stress-test this with `review-artifact`.
