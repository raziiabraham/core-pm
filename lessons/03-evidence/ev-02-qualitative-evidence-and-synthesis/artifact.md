# Artifact template · Insight synthesis

The tutor fills this in with the learner, using the learner's sessions and the
learner's judgment. Never invent a quote, a participant, a session count, or a
theme. Where something is unknown, write `<unknown>` and carry it into the Gaps
section. A named gap is a finding. A fabricated quote is a liability that
survives longer than the person who wrote it.

Delete the guidance in parentheses in the finished artifact.

```markdown
# Insight synthesis · <short topic name>

- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Product:** <product>
- **Decision this serves:** <one sentence>
- **Sessions:** <count> — <format: interview, observed session, support call review>

## Recruiting frame

- **Who was included:** <behaviour and context, not job title alone>
- **How they were reached:** <channel, and who introduced them>
- **Who could not appear in this frame:** <the population this method structurally missed>
- **What that absence could hide:** <one sentence>

## Observations

(One row per observation. Attributable, no cause attached, no adjectives that
imply a mechanism.)

| # | Session | Observation | Type (said / did / showed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <S1> | <what was said or seen> | <type> |

## Patterns

(A pattern is a claim plus its anchors. Count sessions, never quotes.)

### Pattern 1 · <short name>

- **Claim:** <the pattern, stated so it could be wrong>
- **Anchored in:** <session ids> — <n of N sessions>
- **Conditions under which it held:** <context>
- **Interpretation (mine, not the users'):** <proposed mechanism>
- **What would falsify this interpretation:** <observation>

## Contradiction register

(Every observation that argues against a pattern. Do not file these as
outliers. The difference between the contradicting cases and the rest is
usually the real boundary of the finding.)

| Pattern | Contradicting observation | Session | What is different about this case |
|---|---|---|---|
| <pattern> | <observation> | <S7> | <the condition that differs> |

## What this cannot establish

- Prevalence: <state plainly that these sessions cannot size the population>
- Magnitude: <what the cost of the problem is, and why this method cannot price it>
- Causation: <what would be needed instead>

## Position

- **What you now believe:** <one sentence>
- **Strength:** <weak | moderate | strong> — <why, in one line>
- **Strongest reason this is wrong:** <the best argument against your own reading>
- **What you deliberately do not conclude:** <the overreach you are refusing>

## Revision trigger

<The observation that would make you revisit this synthesis — a session from the
excluded population, a behavioural counts check, a workaround that disappears.
Name the observable, not an intention to keep listening.>

## Gaps

<Everything marked `<unknown>` above, listed as open questions with who could
close them.>
```

## Quality bar

Before saving, check all six exposures are present. Name any that are missing
rather than filling them in yourself:

| Exposure | Present when |
|---|---|
| Choice | The decision these sessions serve is named, not just a topic area |
| Evidence | Every pattern has session anchors and a stated recruiting frame |
| Uncertainty | Strength is stated, and prevalence is explicitly not claimed |
| Alternatives | The contradiction register has real entries with their conditions |
| Owner | Interpretations are attributed to you, not voiced as the users' words |
| Revision trigger | A specific observation that would change the reading |

Tell the learner they can stress-test this with `review-artifact`.
