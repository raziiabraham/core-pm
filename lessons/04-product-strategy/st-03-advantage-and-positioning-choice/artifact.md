# Artifact template · Positioning choice

The tutor fills this in with the learner, using the learner's judgment and the
learner's evidence. Never invent a competitor claim, a customer quote, or a
capability the product does not have. If the learner cannot name what is given
up, do not supply it — record the omission and say the position is not yet a
choice.

Delete the guidance in parentheses in the finished artifact.

```markdown
# Positioning choice · <product>

- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Holds until:** <date or condition — positions have durations>
- **Owner:** <who owns this choice>
- **Diagnosis this serves:** <one line from ST-01>

## The position

<One sentence a reasonable colleague could disagree with. It must contain the
audience and the difference.>

## The four commitments

| Commitment | Statement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | <defined by observable behaviour, not by label> | <evidenced / assumed> |
| Alternative | <what they use today, including doing it manually or not at all> | <evidenced / assumed> |
| Valued difference | <better on a dimension this audience already weighs> | <evidenced / assumed> |
| Sustaining capability | <what makes the difference hold> | <live / in design / planned> |

## Why the difference holds

- **Valued because:** <evidence that the audience weighs this dimension>
- **Hard to copy because:** <what makes copying slow, costly, or unattractive>
- **Time to copy, honestly:** <weeks / quarters / years, and who could do it>
- **If the capability is not live:** <state plainly that the position is
  currently a bet, and what would make it real>

## What is given up

| Sacrifice | Specifics |
|---|---|
| Population that now gets a worse product | <who, and worse in what way> |
| Request type declined even with revenue attached | <the type, and the sentence you will say> |
| Capability deliberately not built | <what, and for how long> |

## How others should use this

<Two or three examples of requests, and how a support lead, account manager, or
designer resolves each one using this document alone.>

## Evidence and limits

| Claim | Source | Population | Window | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <claim> | <source> | <who> | <when> | <limit> |

## Uncertainty

- **Confidence:** <low / moderate / high> — <why>
- **Strongest case for the audience you gave up:** <argue it properly>
- **What it would cost to reverse this choice:** <effort, time, relationships>

## Revision trigger

<The observation and threshold that would reopen this choice, and who would see
it first. Include the bridge condition if this audience is a bridge: what
specifically would make you leave it.>

## Gaps

<Everything marked `<unknown>`, as open questions with who could close them.>
```

## Quality bar

Before saving, check all six exposures are present. Name any that are missing
rather than filling them in yourself:

| Exposure | Present when |
|---|---|
| Choice | One audience is committed to, and one is explicitly given up |
| Evidence | The valued difference and the audience definition each cite a source or are marked assumed |
| Uncertainty | Capability status is live, in design, or planned, and stated honestly |
| Alternatives | The audience's real alternative is named, and the rejected audience is argued fairly |
| Owner | The owner of the choice and the people who will apply it are named |
| Revision trigger | An observable and a threshold that would reopen the choice |

Tell the learner they can stress-test this with `review-artifact`.
